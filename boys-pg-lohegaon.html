Premium Boys PG in Lohegaon, Pune - Moonlight Hostel

Welcome to Moonlight Hostel, a premium student PG and boys' coliving space in Lohegaon, Pune. Strategically located near D.Y. Patil University, our accommodation offers students a quiet, focus-oriented sanctuary away from the highway traffic and city noise, helping you maintain a balanced, healthy, and highly productive student lifestyle. We cover all your coliving needs so that you don't have to worry about the daily management of housekeeping, laundry, and meals.

A Dedicated Study Zone: Personal 1:1 Study Desks

Academic performance requires an ergonomic setup. In our premium PG, we reject the practice of sharing study desks. We guarantee a 1:1 student-to-desk ratio. Every single resident receives an individual, spacious wooden workstation, a comfortable high-back study chair, dedicated bookshelves, and direct power plugs next to the bed. This distraction-free workspace allows students to attend online lectures, write software projects, and prepare for competitive engineering exams in absolute comfort and peace.

Fully-Managed Cleaning and Laundry Services

Time is the most valuable resource for a student. To save you time, our professional housekeeping staff sweeps, mops, and sanitizes student bedrooms and attached bathrooms on a daily schedule. In addition, our in-house laundry service washes, dries, and iron-presses your clothes and linens multiple times a week. This service is completely free and included in the yearly bundle, ensuring you never have to waste a Saturday washing heavy jeans or bedsheets by hand.

Healthy Dining: Home-Style Catering Plans

Living away from family shouldn't mean eating unhealthy fast food. Moonlight PG provides a comprehensive home-style dining plan prepared in our hygienic on-site kitchen. Residents receive a wholesome breakfast and a hot dinner every weekday. On Saturdays and Sundays, we serve three complete meals, including a special relaxed Sunday brunch. We use fresh local produce, high-quality dairy, and custom menus designed to support your physical health and overall energy levels.

Uncompromised Security Infrastructure

We believe peace of mind is essential for academic focus. The building is secured by modern biometric access gates, requiring fingerprint scans for entry. Continuous 24/7 CCTV surveillance cameras cover all entryways, lobbies, common dining halls, and corridors. A resident warden resides on-site full-time to coordinate amenities, support students during medical emergencies, and ensure strict compliance with hostel regulations. The facility also features round-the-clock power backup through a high-capacity generator and clean RO drinking water plants.

Spacious Double-Sharing Balcony Rooms

Our rooms are designed to provide maximum ventilation and comfort. We offer two double-sharing configurations. The Balcony Double Sharing Room features sliding glass doors that open to a private balcony, lockable wooden wardrobe cabinets, side-by-side study tables, and a modern western washroom with an attached high-capacity geyser. The Standard Double Sharing Room provides a clean, optimized layout with twin custom beds and built-in cupboards. Both room types offer a secure, premium living environment close to your classes.

Prime Commute and Lohegaon Proximities

Moonlight Hostel is located at Sai Samruddhi Park, Nimbalkar Nagar, Lane No. 6, D.Y. Patil University Road, Lohegaon, Pune - 411047. The hostel is only 1.2 km from the campus, which is just a 3-minute ride or a 12-minute walk. Local convenience stores, print shops, cafes, and medical clinics are located within 200 meters, making the location extremely convenient for students.
