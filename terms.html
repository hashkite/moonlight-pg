Terms and Conditions & Rules - Moonlight Hostel

Welcome to Moonlight Hostel. By booking a room or residing in our premium boys' hostel near D.Y. Patil University in Lohegaon, Pune, you agree to comply with the terms, conditions, and resident rules outlined below to ensure a safe, clean, and focus-oriented community for all students.

Hostel Admission and Fees

Admission is granted on an annual basis. Students must submit the required admission forms, college enrollment verification, and parent/guardian consent agreements. Fees must be paid according to the selected double-sharing room plan, and deposits are required to secure room keys. All booking deposits are non-refundable.

General Code of Conduct

Residents must maintain academic discipline and respect the rights of other students. Silent study hours are enforced daily in common lobbies and bedrooms. Smoking, drinking alcohol, consumption of banned substances, and ragging are strictly prohibited and result in immediate expulsion from the premises.

Security and Access Regulations

Entry to the building is restricted to registered residents and authorized staff via biometric fingerprint verification. Visitors and parents are not allowed in student rooms without written permission from the resident warden. All common areas, entrances, and corridors are monitored 24/7 by CCTV cameras.

Facility Usage and Amenities

Students are responsible for maintaining the cleanliness of their personal workspaces, beds, and attached bathrooms. Housekeeping staff will clean rooms daily. High-speed Wi-Fi, laundry, and dining services must be used responsibly. Any damage to hostel property will be charged directly to the responsible resident.
