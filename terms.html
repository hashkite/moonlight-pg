Terms and Conditions & Resident Rules - Moonlight Hostel

Welcome to Moonlight Hostel. By booking a room or residing in our premium boys' hostel near D.Y. Patil University in Lohegaon, Pune, you agree to comply with the terms, conditions, and resident rules outlined below to ensure a safe, clean, and focus-oriented community for all students. These guidelines are designed to establish a respectful learning environment, protect hostel properties, and guarantee the personal security of every student resident during their academic year.

Hostel Admission, Bookings & Payment Fees

Admission is granted on a strict annual academic basis. To apply, students must submit the official online registration form, college admission proof (from D.Y. Patil University or local institutions), parent or guardian identity verification, and police clearance details. Room booking deposits are required to secure a room layout and key assignments. All fees must be paid in full or according to the agreed payment schedule. Booking deposits, token amounts, and advance payments are strictly non-refundable and non-transferable under any circumstances.

General Code of Conduct & House Discipline

Residents are expected to maintain academic discipline and respect the rights of other students. Silent study hours are enforced daily in all corridors, common study lounges, and bedrooms from 9:30 PM to 6:00 AM. Smoking, drinking alcohol, gambling, and consumption of banned substances are strictly prohibited inside the building and gates. Moonlight Hostel observes a zero-tolerance policy against ragging, physical harassment, or verbal abuse, which results in immediate expulsion without fee refunds.

Security, Access Control & CCTV Surveillance

Student safety is our top priority. Entry to the building is restricted strictly to registered student residents and authorized staff via biometric fingerprint verification scanners. Visitors, including friends, classmates, and parents, are not allowed inside student bedrooms without written permission from the resident warden. All corridors, entrances, lobbies, and common areas are monitored 24/7 by CCTV security cameras. Residents must cooperate with warden check-ins and emergency safety drills.

Facility Maintenance, Rooms & Utilities

Residents must maintain their personal study workstations, custom beds, and attached washrooms in a clean, hygienic state. Housekeeping staff will clean bedrooms and bathrooms daily, and laundry services are run multiple times a week. However, students are responsible for safety checks of their electronic devices. Heavy appliances (such as private induction stoves or heaters) are not permitted in rooms to prevent electrical fire hazards. Any damage to furniture, window screens, or common sports equipment will be charged directly to the responsible student.

Dining Hall and RO Water Regulations

Dining hall meals are served during designated timings only. Students must maintain cleanliness in the dining hall and avoid wasting food. Weekday catering includes breakfast and dinner, while weekend plans cover breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Outside guests are not permitted to dine in the hostel mess without booking coupons. High-stage RO drinking water plants located on each floor must be used responsibly, and wasting water is strictly prohibited.

Contact our Warden Office

For any queries regarding the terms of service, resident rules, or room clearances, please contact our resident warden Somnath Bhalsing directly at +91 92701 32323, or visit the warden registration office on the ground floor.
