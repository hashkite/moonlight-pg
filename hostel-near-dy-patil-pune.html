Premium Boys' Hostel near D.Y. Patil University, Pune

Looking for the best boys' hostel or PG near D.Y. Patil University (DYPU) in Pune? Moonlight Hostel is your ideal choice, located just 1.2 km from the university campus gates in Lohegaon. We offer premium, secure, and fully-managed accommodation options designed specifically for college students who value a peaceful environment, comfortable rooms, and home-style catering services.

Quick Commute to D.Y. Patil Campus

Our prime location on D.Y. Patil University Road means you can reach your classes in a 3-minute ride or a 12-minute walk. We also offer an optional daily shuttle transit service directly from the hostel doorstep to the main university campus gates to make your daily academic commute fast, safe, and completely hassle-free, saving you precious time for studying.

High-Focus Study Environment: 1:1 Personal Workstations

We provide each student with a dedicated 1:1 workstation, complete with a spacious wooden desk, comfortable high-back study chair, dedicated bookshelves, and direct power sockets next to their bed. The hostel observes designated silent study hours in common lobbies to ensure a peaceful space that boosts academic focus and productivity during exams.

Fully-Managed Services & Utilities

Focus entirely on your academic curriculum while we handle the rest. We provide professional daily housekeeping, free laundry services multiple times a week, high-speed fiber Wi-Fi, and 24/7 generator power backup. Healthy, home-style breakfast and dinner are served daily by our on-site warden team, using fresh ingredients to support your health.

Safety, Biometric Security & Student Recreation

The premises are equipped with secure card-key and biometric fingerprint access gates, continuous CCTV monitoring, and a resident warden who resides on-site to assist students. For recreation, students have access to common study and dining tables, indoor chess tables, carrom zones, and table tennis to relax after classes.
